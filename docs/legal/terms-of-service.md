## 7. Your responsibilities and the authenticity of your evidence

Your records are only as good as what you put in. You agree that:

- **Be truthful.** What you upload will be a genuine, honest record of what it claims to show.
- **Don't falsify.** You won't edit, stage, fabricate, backdate or otherwise misrepresent a photo, a document, a note or a date, and you won't use AI or editing tools to create or alter evidence. You won't present someone else's rental or a different time as yours.
- **Check everything.** You'll review every report before relying on it or sharing it. Reports are generated from your own input, and you're responsible for their accuracy.
- **Be complete enough.** You decide what to document. MoveMark's suggestions and progress indicators don't mean your record is complete.
- **Follow the rules.** You'll follow your lease and the law, including any rules about entering spaces, recording people, deadlines and notice. MoveMark doesn't track your legal deadlines for you.

**What MoveMark does and doesn't record.** You should understand the limits of the record:

- **Capture time.** This comes from the metadata in the photo file. It's declared by the file, and can be wrong or changed. **We don't verify it.** If a photo has no capture time, the report uses the time we received it.
- **Received time.** This is the time our server recorded the photo. It shows when we got the file, not when the photo was taken.
- **Source.** We record whether a photo came from the in-app camera or from your library. A library photo may have been taken or edited at any time, by anyone.
- **Fingerprint.** We store a SHA-256 fingerprint of each original when it's uploaded through the current apps. It can show whether a file has changed since then. It can't show that the photo was truthful, or when it was really taken.
- **Reports.** Report captions show one time per photo, in UTC. [CONFLICT C6: captions don't currently say whether that's the capture time or the received time, or whether the photo came from the camera or library.] A report's photos are working copies, not originals.
- **Older records** may not have an original or a fingerprint.

MoveMark doesn't check the truth of any record, and neither we nor our records can vouch for you. Falsifying evidence can have serious legal consequences for you.
