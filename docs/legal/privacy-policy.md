# MoveMark Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

MoveMark ("MoveMark," "we," "our," or "us") provides tools to document rental property condition, maintenance, move-in/move-out records, and export evidence. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Information We Collect
- Account info (name, email, auth identifiers)
- Property/workflow data (addresses, room records, notes, logs)
- Evidence content (photos, files, supporting records)
- Subscription status and entitlement metadata (Apple/RevenueCat)
- Diagnostics needed for reliability and security

## How We Use Information
- Operate account login and profile
- Store/sync property evidence and records
- Generate exports and dispute-support documents
- Enforce plan limits and paid entitlements
- Provide support and improve app reliability

## Sharing
We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run MoveMark (e.g. backend/auth/storage/subscriptions), or when required by law/security/compliance.

## Permissions
- Camera: capture condition evidence
- Photo Library: attach existing evidence and supporting images

## Retention and Deletion
We retain data as needed to provide the service and meet legal/security obligations. Account deletion requests can be sent to support.

## Contact
- Support: support@movemark.app

## Updates
We may update this policy; material changes will update the effective date.
