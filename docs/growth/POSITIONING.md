# Positioning

## Initial audience (v1)

**Ontario apartment renters** moving into a new rental — especially first-time renters worried about being blamed for pre-existing damage.

Not “all renters everywhere” at launch. Sharpen messaging, word-of-mouth, and ad targeting in one market first.

## Core message

**Headline:** Prove what was already there.

**Subhead:** Take room photos before you unpack. Turn them into a report when you need it.

**Pain line:** Old damage can cost you your deposit — unless you have proof.

## Transformation (sell this, not features)

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| “I might get blamed for old damage.” | “I have proof saved before anything goes wrong.” |
| Random camera roll | Timestamped room-by-room report |
| Arguing at move-out | PDF + dispute packet when questioned |

## Pricing hypothesis

Would renters pay **$4.99–$9.99/month** to protect a **$1,500–$3,000** deposit?

Test with Meta ads + paywall conversion before scaling spend.

## Free vs Pro

| Free | Pro |
|------|-----|
| 1 proof vault | Unlimited vaults |
| 1 move-in report | More reports + move-out |
| Move-in room proof | Move-out proof + dispute packet |
