# Marketing content system

Sell the **transformation**, not the app checklist.

## Five ad / content concepts

1. **Before you unpack** — “Take these 12 photos before moving into any rental.”
2. **Already there** — “Prove damage was already there when you moved in.”
3. **Camera roll isn’t enough** — “Your landlord needs timestamps and room labels.”
4. **The $900 charge** — “The wall damage that could’ve been avoided with move-in proof.”
5. **Move-out proof** — “Re-capture rooms before you return the keys.”

## Short-form hooks (TikTok / Reels)

- POV: landlord says you caused the scratch → you open MoveMark report  
- Timer: 5 minutes, one room, Kitchen first  
- Split screen: boxes covering floor vs clear move-in photos  

## Meta image ad copy (starter)

**Primary:** Prove what was already there.  
**Headline:** Document your apartment before you unpack.  
**CTA:** Learn more / Install  

**Variant (fear):** Don’t lose your deposit to old damage.  

## Reddit post angle

Title: *Moving into a Toronto apartment? Take photos in every room before you unpack.*

Body: Brief story + “I use room-by-room proof and a PDF if they question the deposit” — no hard sell; ask what others do.

## Email / waitlist follow-up

Subject: *Before you unpack — 5-minute move-in proof*

Body: Link to app / TestFlight when ready; one CTA: start with Kitchen.
