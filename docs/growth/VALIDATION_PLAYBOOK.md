# Validation playbook

Run before scaling ads or store spend.

## Assets to build

- [ ] Landing waitlist (`/landing/index.html`) live on GitHub Pages
- [ ] 5 Meta ad concepts (see [MARKETING_CONTENT.md](./MARKETING_CONTENT.md))
- [ ] App Store screenshots (see [APP_STORE_SCREENSHOTS.md](./APP_STORE_SCREENSHOTS.md))
- [ ] Reddit / TikTok comment research (renter move-in threads)
- [ ] 10 renter interviews or DMs

## Small tests ($20–$50)

1. **Meta traffic** → landing waitlist or App Store product page  
2. **Reddit post** (r/Toronto, r/renters) — “Before you unpack, document this”  
3. **Pricing** — survey: “Would you pay $X to protect your deposit?”

## Success signals

- Waitlist signups at &lt; $5 CPA (test)
- Interviewees say they’ve been charged for old damage or fear it
- Paywall tap → purchase (or strong “would pay” in interviews)

## What not to do yet

- Scale broad “all renters” ads
- Buy Google Play only for billing — validate demand first
- Add features before onboarding/paywall convert
