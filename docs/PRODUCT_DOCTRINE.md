# MoveMark Product Doctrine

MoveMark exists to remove the hassle and uncertainty of protecting yourself as a renter.

The renter should never have to decide how to organize rental evidence, remember what they captured, search their camera roll, or put a record together by hand. MoveMark tells the renter what proof is missing, organizes what they capture, keeps the evidence, and turns it into professional records they can share when needed.

**Positioning:** MoveMark helps renters protect themselves by turning ordinary rental evidence into organized proof.

## We optimize for

- Protection over feature count
- Evidence over decoration
- Clarity over complexity
- Fewer decisions over more options
- Trust over novelty
- Delivered artifacts over engagement metrics

## Decision filter

Every change must do at least one of these: strengthen renter protection, reduce user effort, improve evidence quality, improve trust, or make the business more sustainable. Ask three questions:

1. Does this make the renter feel more protected?
2. Does this remove hassle?
3. Does this make the value easier to understand?

## Scope: MoveMark owns rental evidence

These are the existing modules: Rooms · Photos · Documents · Maintenance · Move-in evidence · Move-out evidence · Reports · Dispute packet.

A new idea must strengthen one of these modules. Rent payments, landlord messaging, AI legal advice, roommate management and listings are out of scope.

## Messaging layers

| Layer | Message |
|---|---|
| Emotional | Protect yourself before there's a dispute. |
| Functional | Capture and organize rental evidence. |
| Artifact | Generate professional move-in, move-out, and dispute records. |
| Proof | Photos, dates, documents, issues, and reports stay connected. |

Social content sells the emotional layer. The app itself stays calm and professional.

## Copy rules

- Progress is **coverage** ("1 of 10 rooms documented"), never a score or a percent-done. (This is already the rule in `MMVaultProofHeroCard`.)
- Name the next room when we know it ("Capture Living Room"), not a generic "Continue".
- A finished report is a **record**, not a file ("Your evidence record is ready", not "PDF ready").
- Sell Pro by its outcome (a complete record from move-in to move-out), then list features.
- Never imply legal advice.

## Funnel and activation metric

Sees content → visits site / store → installs → creates rental → **captures first room → captures second room** → generates first report → sees Pro value → subscribes → returns during tenancy → uses move-out.

**Activation = % of new users who document at least 2 rooms.** Installs and account creation don't count as activation.

## Experiment template

Every meaningful post-launch change uses this template:

```text
PROBLEM          Only 25% of users capture a second room.
HYPOTHESIS       Naming the next room removes decision friction.
CHANGE           "Continue documenting" → "Capture Living Room"
SUCCESS METRIC   Second-room completion within 24 hours.
RESULT           25% → 37%
DECISION         Keep.
```
